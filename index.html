Case study: coordinating multiple projects and teams

At a remote marketing agency, I supported the founder in coordinating multiple client projects and cross-functional teams. I used ClickUp to organize tasks, clarify responsibilities, track deadlines, monitor progress, and keep outstanding deliverables visible.

The challenge

Multiple projects ran at once, with developers, marketers, closers, and appointment setters each owning different deliverables. Without an organized tracking and follow-up process, it was hard to see progress, spot outstanding tasks, and keep everyone aligned.

What I did

  • Built and maintained ClickUp task structures for projects, responsibilities, deadlines, and statuses
  • Used trackers, checklists, and progress reports to monitor deliverables and pending items
  • Followed up with team members on a consistent routine
  • Sent progress updates to the founder
  • Documented recurring processes as SOPs and step-by-step instructions

The result

  • Clearer visibility into progress, pending deliverables, and who was responsible for what
  • A more organized way for the founder to monitor work without personally chasing every task
  • More structured coordination and more consistent progress tracking

Scope of coordination

Multiple concurrent projects and cross-functional team members across development, marketing, closing, and appointment setting. This was project and workflow coordination, not direct people management.

My contribution to operational visibility

  • Progress on each project, and what was still pending
  • Who was responsible for which deliverable
  • Which items needed a follow-up
  • Regular updates so the founder could monitor work without chasing every task
